## Lo más destacado

- **Tú decides cuánto guarda esta Mac.** Lo que recibes y envías se queda en esta Mac **30 días** desde la fecha del envío, o lo que elijas: 7 días, 90 días, 1 año o nunca. Lo que ya tenías **no se borra hasta dentro de 7 días**, para que te dé tiempo a cambiarlo. Nunca toca lo que pasaste a un agente ni los archivos que aún no has visto.
- **Tus mensajes, en todos tus dispositivos.** Pulsar Cloud **espera a cada uno de tus dispositivos** antes de vaciar su buzón: lo que te llega lo guardan tu Mac y tu iPhone aunque uno estuviera apagado, y lo que envías desde uno aparece en los demás. Lo leído en uno se marca leído en todos.

## Highlights

- **You decide how long this Mac keeps things.** What you receive and send stays on this Mac for **30 days** from the date it was sent, or whatever you choose: 7 days, 90 days, 1 year or never. What you already had **isn’t deleted for another 7 days**, so you have time to change it. It never touches what you handed to an agent or files you haven’t seen yet.
- **Your messages on all your devices.** Pulsar Cloud **waits for each of your devices** before emptying its mailbox: what you receive is saved on your Mac and your iPhone even if one was off, and what you send from one shows up on the others. Read on one, read on all.

## Good to know

- _Team_ · This Mac starts deleting its copy of shares older than **30 days** **7 days after you update**. Change it in **Settings › Account and team › Copy on this Mac**; with “Never” nothing is deleted.

## New

- _Team_ · **Copy on this Mac** (Settings › Account and team): how long received and sent items are kept (7 days, 30, 90, 1 year or never), how much space they take and where they are.
- _Team chat_ · What you’re sent reaches **all your active devices** (used in the last 14 days) while it’s still in Pulsar Cloud, up to 30 days: before, the first one to save it left the others just 24 hours. **Your devices** (Settings › Account and team) lists each one and lets you remove one you no longer use.

## Improvements

- _Team chat_ · What you **send from your iPhone or another Mac** shows up on this one, and what you delete for everyone from another device leaves its placeholder here. What you read on another device is marked read right away.

## Fixes

- _Team chat_ · When your **Mac wakes** or you come back to Pulsar, the inbox and sent items reload: alerts lost while the connection was down no longer leave messages unseen. And items beyond the first 50 shares are no longer left out.
- _Performance_ · Pulsar **no longer freezes when you enlarge the window** or switch displays: when macOS restarts the display refresh, terminals keep drawing instead of waiting forever.
