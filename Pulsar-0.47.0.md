Como su sandbox no deja a la terminal hablar con Pulsar, usa la herramienta `pulsar` del panel (la misma que Codex), que respeta tus reglas de `agy`; en el primer turno puede no estar disponible todavía.

Sus **diálogos de aprobación** de órdenes y archivos también se responden desde ahí; si no se ve entera la orden, desde el iPhone solo se puede rechazar.

## New

- _Agents_ · **Antigravity CLI now works like Claude and Codex.** It gets Pulsar's guidance in every pane, receives messages from other agents with `pulsar agent send` (even when idle) and the text you type from the iPhone. Since its sandbox doesn't let the terminal talk to Pulsar, it uses the pane's `pulsar` tool (the same one Codex uses), which respects your `agy` rules; it may not be available yet on the first turn.
- _Agents_ · **Antigravity's questions** show up in the answer bar and on the iPhone with their exact options: up to four questions in a row, multiple choice and answers in your own words. Its **approval dialogs** for commands and files can be answered from there too; if the whole command isn't visible, the iPhone can only decline.

## Improvements

- _Sidebar_ · The **Limits** row shows **Antigravity**'s: the five-hour and seven-day windows for Gemini and for other models, and the plan. Each Antigravity pane also shows how much context it has used.
- _Agents_ · Work modes support **xhigh** effort on the Antigravity models that have it.

## Fixes

- _Browser_ · A page that **crashed while loading** no longer makes the browser flicker endlessly: after two reloads in a row Pulsar stops, says the page closed on its own and lets you reload it. Popups (a sign-in) shown in the viewer no longer stay blank.
