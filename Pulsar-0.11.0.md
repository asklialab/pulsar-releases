## Lo más destacado

- **Una ventana nueva: quién lanzó a quién, de un vistazo.** Cada agente y las tareas que lanzó forman una **familia** con su tono, unida por **ramas** que corren por los huecos entre paneles, sin gastar espacio. Caben **hasta 9 terminales** con el navegador y el visor. Si prefieres la de antes, está en **Ajustes › Ventana**.
- **Arriba, solo quien trabaja o te espera.** Quien termina enseña su ✓ un momento y baja a la **repisa**; quien vuelve a trabajar sube solo. Con **Fijo** nadie se mueve salvo quien te pregunta. Arrastra una familia por su orquestador para cambiar el orden.
- **Responde sin buscar la pregunta.** El panel que te espera se marca en ámbar y lleva la pregunta y sus opciones **al pie**: un clic responde. **⌥⌘↵** salta a la siguiente, también en otros espacios.
- **El chat, un panel más.** El chat se abre a la derecha como un panel más y su hilo va **en ramas**: cada respuesta **brota de su mensaje**. Lo que pasas a un agente **cuelga del mensaje** con su estado en vivo y una rama lo une a su panel.
- **Tu equipo, arriba junto al chat.** Una **cápsula** con quien te escribió y quien está sustituye al botón del chat. Lo que te envían **cae de ella** y se lo pasas a un agente con un clic; todo lo de hoy queda en la **galería**: clic para verlo en grande, **arrástralo** a un agente para pasárselo.

## Highlights

- **A new window: who launched whom, at a glance.** Each agent and the tasks it launched form a **family** with its own tone, joined by **branches** that run through the gaps between panes, without using any space. Up to **9 terminals** fit alongside the browser and the viewer. If you prefer the old one, it’s in **Settings › Window**.
- **Up top, only agents working or waiting on you.** Agents that finish show their ✓ for a moment and move down to the **shelf**; agents that start working again come back up on their own. With **Fixed**, nothing moves except agents asking you something. Drag a family by its orchestrator to change the order.
- **Answer without hunting for the question.** The pane waiting on you is outlined in amber and shows the question and its options **at the bottom**: one click answers. **⌥⌘↵** jumps to the next one, in other spaces too.
- **Chat, just another pane.** Chat opens on the right as just another pane, and its thread is laid out **in branches**: each reply **grows from its message**. Whatever you hand to an agent **hangs from the message** with its live status, and a branch joins it to that agent’s pane.
- **Your team, up top next to the chat.** A **capsule** with who wrote to you and who’s around replaces the chat button. What they send you **drops from it** and you hand it to an agent with one click; everything from today stays in the **gallery**: click to see it large, **drag it** onto an agent to hand it over.

## Good to know

- _Settings_ · The classic window stays available in **Settings › Window** for a while.

## New

- _Agents_ · Top bar with the status of all your agents: a meter, how many are working, waiting or done, and one click takes you to the next.
- _Panes_ · The browser and the viewer sit together on the “desk”, with one tab per pane; with 9 terminals they fold into live thumbnails that open on top without moving anything.
- _Panes_ · **⌘↵** maximizes the focused pane (so do ⌘⇧↩ and double-clicking its header).
- _Team_ · Gallery of everything received in the last 24 h, by time or by person and by type, with a large view that plays videos, shows pages without running their JavaScript and puts 2 or 3 alternatives **side by side**.
- _Team_ · You can see when someone is **typing** to you, on their face and at the bottom of the conversation.

## Improvements

- _Settings_ · Refreshed sidebar: each space shows its agents’ status and pending question, and the task tree uses the same tones as the branches.
- _Team_ · “Send to…” and your teams move to the chat bar; cloud notices go to the sidebar notice line, which no longer holds the team area.
