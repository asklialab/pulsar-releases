## Lo más destacado

- **Compartir en vivo.** Comparte **la página que estás haciendo** (tu `localhost`) sin subirla a ningún sitio: pulsa **Compartir** en la barra del navegador y elige **Solo yo** (tu iPhone), **tu equipo** (o algunas personas) o un **enlace de Pulsar**. Va **cifrada de punta a punta** desde tu Mac y tú decides quién entra: cada dispositivo nuevo te pide **Admitir** con su huella. En el chat del equipo aparece una tarjeta con su estado; quien la recibe la abre con **Ver** en su visor, aislada, o con **Revisar con un agente**. Las cuentas sin plan pueden **mirar** lo que les comparten.
- **Pulsar desde Codex y Claude.** Conecta Pulsar a **Codex**, **Claude** o **Claude Code** desde **Ajustes › Integraciones** y pídeles desde su chat que **lancen agentes de Pulsar**, sigan su progreso, respondan sus preguntas y te enseñen lo que hacen. La primera vez **tú decides en la Mac** hasta dónde llegan: «Ver», «Operar» (solo los agentes que lanzan ellos) o «Total», que pide Touch ID o tu contraseña.

## A tener en cuenta

- _Navegador_ · `pulsar browser` fuera de un panel de Pulsar (y `pulsar mcp` para el navegador) **pasa por el permiso de Integraciones**: un cliente solo maneja los navegadores que abre él.
- _Agentes_ · `pulsar agent` **solo funciona dentro de un panel** de Pulsar; desde fuera, usa `pulsar mcp`.
- _Integraciones_ · Si mueves Pulsar de carpeta, al abrirla **repite sola la instalación en Codex** que ya aceptaste, con la ruta nueva; lo demás te lo pide en **Ajustes › Integraciones**.

## Highlights

- **Share live.** Share **the page you're building** (your `localhost`) without uploading it anywhere: press **Share** in the browser bar and choose **Only me** (your iPhone), **your team** (or some people) or a **Pulsar link**. It's **end-to-end encrypted** from your Mac and you decide who gets in: each new device asks you to **Admit** it, with its fingerprint. A card with its status shows up in the team chat; whoever gets it opens it with **View** in their viewer, isolated, or with **Review with an agent**. Accounts without a plan can **watch** what's shared with them.
- **Pulsar from Codex and Claude.** Connect Pulsar to **Codex**, **Claude** or **Claude Code** from **Settings › Integrations** and ask them from their chat to **launch Pulsar agents**, follow their progress, answer their questions and show you what they make. The first time, **you decide on the Mac** how far they go: “View”, “Operate” (only the agents they launch) or “Full”, which asks for Touch ID or your password.

## Good to know

- _Browser_ · `pulsar browser` outside a Pulsar pane (and `pulsar mcp` for the browser) **goes through the Integrations permission**: a client only drives the browsers it opens.
- _Agents_ · `pulsar agent` **only works inside a Pulsar pane**; from outside, use `pulsar mcp`.
- _Integrations_ · If you move Pulsar to another folder, when it opens it **repeats on its own the Codex install** you already accepted, with the new path; everything else it asks you in **Settings › Integrations**.

## New

- _Share live_ · Your agents can **ask you** to share a port with `pulsar share port`; they never share it with other people on their own: you confirm on the Mac.
- _Share live_ · **Review with an agent** opens Claude in an empty folder, with its own isolated browser and in a mode that asks: what it reads from the page belongs to someone else and it can't touch your projects or your other agents.
- _Share live_ · **Settings › Share live**: the devices you admitted (with “Remove”), the blocked ones and who watched.
- _Security_ · Buttons only a person can press (Admit, Share with the team…) are confirmed with **Touch ID or your password** when you use them with VoiceOver, the keyboard or Voice Control.
- _Integrations_ · In Claude's chat (and Codex's, where its app shows panels), a **live panel** with your agents: what's waiting for you on top, questions with buttons and thumbnails of what they show.
- _Integrations_ · If a client tries to accept a permission its level doesn't allow, the **answer bar** tells you and you decide.

## Improvements

- _Appearance_ · In light mode, **terminal colors are easy to read**: light tones darken just enough on the daytime background.

## Fixes
