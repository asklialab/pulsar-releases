## Highlights

- **Try Lumbre, the new look.** More room, fewer borders and what's waiting for you, in the Island. Your agents, spaces and shortcuts stay where they were. **Classic is still the default**; Lumbre is a preview and you can switch back whenever you like.
- **Meetings without words nobody said.** Transcripts no longer fill up with noise: **the TV or people in the background**, the “Mm-hmm” and “Yeah” that came out of the call and **your own voice echoed back by the call** are left out. If you switch language yourself at your usual volume, it is still written.
- **The agent's browser doesn't steal your focus.** When an agent opens its browser or asks you for something in it, it **doesn't switch workspace, doesn't unmaximize what you have maximized and doesn't take the keyboard**. Its 🌐 tells you if it's asking for something.
- **Handoffs without filling the Shelf.** The new agent carries on with the task and **the previous session is kept in its pane**. If you need it, open it with “Open Previous Session” in the pane menu.

## New

- _Appearance_ · **Lumbre**, the new look, as a preview: try it from What's New or in Settings. Classic is still the default.

## Improvements

- _Dictation_ · Meeting transcripts no longer fill up with words nobody said: **the TV or people in the background**, the “Mm-hmm” and “Yeah” that came out of call noise and **your own voice echoed back by the call** are left out. If you switch language yourself at your usual volume, it is still written.
- _Agents_ · Context handoff no longer fills the Shelf with hibernated “· previous” agents: the new agent carries on with the task and **the previous session is kept in its pane**. If you need it, open it with “Open Previous Session” in the pane menu; when you close it, it goes back there. The “· previous” agents you already had asleep are tidied up automatically when Pulsar opens.
- _Agents_ · Work modes now offer **GPT-6.1 Terra** for Codex, between Sol and Luna. The built-in modes don't change.
- _Integrations_ · The `pulsar` CLI help is shorter: `help` gives an index with the names, `help --all` the full help and `help a b` several actions at once. Agents spend less context reading it.

## Fixes

- _Dictation_ · In a meeting without headphones, what you said is no longer deleted as if it were echo when the call sent your voice back.
- _Dictation_ · Dictating with **Fn** or asking with **⌥ Fn** during a meeting no longer mutes the Mac's audio (with “Mute audio while dictating” on): you keep hearing everyone else.
- _Browser_ · Agents no longer steal your focus when they use their browser: opening it, asking you for something in it or a popup from their page **don't switch workspace, don't unmaximize what you have maximized and don't take the keyboard**. Their card turns to the web when you let go of their terminal, and their 🌐 tells you if they're asking for something.
