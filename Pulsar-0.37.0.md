## Highlights

- **Meetings with more than 4 people.** Meetings transcribed by **Pulsar Voice** no longer stop at “Speaker 1…4” and “Others”: a **speaker review** rereads the audio every few minutes and when you stop, and fixes the lines already written without losing the names you set. In our tests, a 7-person meeting went from 63% to 91% of lines with the right person, and a 9-person one comes out with all 9. It all runs on your Mac; other people's audio only exists while the meeting lasts.
- **ChatGPT and Claude remember their permission.** The permission you give **ChatGPT** or **Claude** to use Pulsar is no longer lost when you quit Pulsar, the app or update: it is kept in the keychain and what comes back never goes beyond **Operate**. The permission sheet stays centered and still, above other windows, so its buttons work on the first click.

## Good to know

- _Dictation_ · The meetings privacy notice shows once more: it now explains that other people's audio is kept in a nameless temporary file while the meeting lasts (4 hours at most) and is deleted when it ends.
